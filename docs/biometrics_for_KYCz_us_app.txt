Video & Face Liveness Detection
Multi-signal analysis from the video feed detects genuine human presence and defeats spoofing
attempts including printed photos, screen replays, and 3D masks.

Eye Blink Rate Detection
Natural blink patterns measured via Eye
Aspect Ratio (EAR) analysis from 68-point
facial landmarks. Detects absence of blinking
in static images and unnatural blink rates.

Heartbeat Detection (rPPG)
Remote photoplethysmography extracts
pulse signal from subtle skin color changes in
the forehead region. FFT analysis isolates
cardiac rhythm in the 48-180 BPM range.

3D Parallax Analysis
Detects natural depth variation in face
movement that flat screens and printed
photos cannot replicate. Measures inter-frame
displacement patterns for 3D presence proof.

Face Micro-Movements
Subtle involuntary facial movements that
distinguish live faces from static images.
Analyzes frame-to-frame landmark drift for
natural micro-motion signatures.

Face Movement Challenge
Random prompts to turn head left/right, look
up/down, nod, or blink on command. Verifies
real-time control and responsiveness of a live
subject.

Signal Quality & Consistency
Continuous monitoring of rPPG signal quality,
BPM consistency across windows, and
frequency peak prominence to ensure reliable
measurements throughout the session.

5-Step Verification Workflow
1 System Check

Camera access, video quality, face detection and framing, lighting assessment, and FPS validation.
Ensures optimal conditions before verification begins.

2 ID Document Scan

ID card, passport, or driver license capture (front and back) with OCR extraction. AI-powered field
validation and auto-detection of document type.

3 Liveness Detection

rPPG pulse detection, eye blink rate, 3D parallax, micro-movements, face movement challenges, and
speech verification run simultaneously for multi-factor proof of human presence.

4 Chat Verification

AI chatbot questionnaire for onboarding data aggregation. Cross-references extracted document data
with live user responses for identity confirmation.

5 Complete

Comprehensive verification report generated with all factor scores, document data, and session
metadata. Downloadable as PDF or JSON for audit trails.

Privacy by Design
BlockSign Verify is built on a zero-storage architecture. No video frames, images, or biometric
templates are ever stored or transmitted. All processing happens in real-time with ephemeral data
that is discarded immediately after the session ends.
Principle
No Video Storage

Implementation
All frames processed in memory only, never written to disk

No Biometric Templates

No facial embeddings, voiceprints, or fingerprints saved

Ephemeral Processing

Time-series data exists only during active session

Session Isolation

Each verification session is independent and fully discarded

GDPR & CCPA

Full compliance with data protection regulations

Audit Reports

Verification outcome reports without raw biometric data
